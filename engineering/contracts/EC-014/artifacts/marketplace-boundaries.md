# Marketplace Boundaries

## Incluye

Stores, catalogs, products, categories, variants, SKU, pricing visibility and storefront experience.

## Excluye

Identity/IAM (EC-010A), multi-tenancy foundation (EC-010B), payments, shipping, billing, notification infrastructure and customer identity.

## Integración

Consume identidad y contexto tenant; publica eventos de Store y Product mediante contratos compartidos y Kafka.
